    Family Handbook

    Patient & Family Guide

    Essential guidelines for family members before and during hospital stay:

    🧾 What is Needed for Admission?

    • Doctor's prescription or admission slip
    • Patient's government photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter card)
    • Phone number and address proof of guardian / attendant
    • All prior medical reports, prescriptions and test history
    Official guidelines to be verified with Bellview management

    🎒 What Should You Bring?

    • Comfortable loose cotton clothing for the patient
    • Personal hygiene items (towel, soap, brush, mug)
    • Currently ongoing medicine bottles or prescription slips
    • Mobile phone and charger (please avoid bringing expensive jewellery)
    Official guidelines to be verified with Bellview management

    👨‍👩‍👧 Rules for Family & Attendants

    • Usually one authorized attendant is permitted to stay with the inpatient
    • Visiting Hours: 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM [Verification Pending]
    • Please maintain quietness for patients' rest and recovery
    • Bringing young children inside ward areas is discouraged
    Official guidelines to be verified with Bellview management

    📋 Discharge Guidelines

    • Discharge begins after attending doctor rounds in the morning
    • Billing clearance is typically done between 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM
    • Collect discharge summary file and verify dosage schedule
    • Confirm next follow-up consultation date before leaving
    Official guidelines to be verified with Bellview management
