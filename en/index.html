How to reach

Address: {{address}}

  1. Come to Tamluk

    By train, get off at Tamluk railway station. By bus, get off in Tamluk.

  2. Take a toto or auto to Dharinda

    Ask for "Bellview Nursing Home, Vivekananda Nagar, Dharinda", or show the driver this address.

  3. Come to the reception counter

    Give the patient's name and the reason for your visit.
